# MixTogether brand

## Positioning

MixTogether is a private prize-savings pool: calm enough to trust with savings,
playful enough to make a five-minute demo memorable.

- Primary tagline: **Private savings. Provable chances.**
- Supporting line: **Save together. Win in secret.**
- Voice: warm, plain-spoken, privacy-literate, never breathless.

## Typography and shape

Fraunces is the display face. Plus Jakarta Sans is the UI face. Surfaces use
generous radii, solid fills, fine violet borders, and restrained glow. Body
copy never uses gradients. Motion should feel buoyant and slow, and must honor
reduced-motion preferences.

## Product language

Say “savings,” “private balance,” and “draw.” Avoid “bet,” “jackpot,” countdown
pressure, fabricated odds, and claims of wallet anonymity. State clearly that
shield and unshield amounts, wallet participation, and transaction timing are
public.
